Visual shell is textbook dark-SaaS (sticky blurred nav, near-black bg, one gradient accent, glassy cards, marquee, 3-card grid, accordion FAQ) — swap the copy and it could sell any API product. # Overall Impression

A disciplined, honest landing page whose interactive proof (real scanner) and founder transparency are genuinely rare — undermined by a P0 lead-corruption flaw in the scanner's second stage, an amputated mobile nav, and a payment moment that leaves the buyer's most practical question unanswered.

# What's Working

1. The hero scanner: real diagnostic, green/red findings pre-translated into consequence language, 6 locales. Proof-of-competence disguised as a lead magnet.
2. Founder section: real name, LinkedIn, QA framing, self-published mediocre starting score (53/100) — disarming and cheap trust.
3. Copy discipline: no fake urgency/counters/logos; calculator has an honest disclaimer; restraint reads as credibility.
